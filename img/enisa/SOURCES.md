- **`reg-02-csirt-select.png`** — from the drop-down menu and click “Continue”.
- **`reg-03-personal-details.png`** — (First Name, Last Name, Email, Legal name) click “Continue”
- **`reg-04-manufacturer-details.png`** — (manufacturer name, manufacturer adress, additional information) and click “Continue”.
- **`reg-05-invited-personal-details.png`** — (First Name, Last Name, Email, Legal name) and click “Continue”.
- **`ew-01-new-notification.png`** — . A new notification will be automatically created and pre-filled with the user type (assigned representative, AR).
- **`ew-02-early-warning-tab.png`** — and any optional fields needed to describe the notification under the Early Warning tab.
- **`ew-03-select-manufacturer.png`** — (approved or pending validation) or add a new one.
- **`ew-04-additional-notes.png`** — fill in the Additional Notes
- **`if-01-personal-details.png`** — Personal details:
- **`ew-05-before-csirt-validation.png`** — Before CSIRT validation:
- **`ew-06-after-csirt-validation.png`** — After CSIRT validation:
- **`n72-01-open-notification.png`** — on the Dashboard.
- **`fin-01-open-notification.png`** — on the Dashboard.
- **`if-02-alerts-tab.png`** — Open the Alerts tab
- **`if-03-manufacturer-details.png`** — Manufacturer details:
- **`if-04-dashboard.png`** — Access the Dashboard.
- **`if-05-invite-secondary.png`** — and click “Send Invitation”.
- **`if-06-invite-confirmation.png`** — Pop-up confirming the invitation:
- **`if-07-add-manufacturer.png`** — and click on “Save”.
- **`if-08-claim-primary-role.png`** — role for the manufacturer.
- **`if-09-remove-association.png`** — and confirm the action.
- **`pec-01-toggle.png`** — in the 72hrs report template scroll down and toggle the Particular Exceptional Circumstances (PEC) indicator.
- **`pec-02-delay-reasons.png`** — By toggling the indicator, the PEC Delay Reson options will be displayed.

## From the AR User Manual (PDF), added 2026-09-10

ENISA published a *CRA SRP – AR User Manual* on 9 September 2026 (PDF, 55
pages, "Version: 1.1" on every page, document history listing only v1.0):
<https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/product-security/single-reporting-platform-srp/cra-srp-ar-user-manual>.
The ten images below are extracted from that PDF unchanged (the embedded
bitmaps, not page renders). Same licence as above — ENISA material, source
acknowledged beside each image on the guide. They show a test tenant
("Test Test", "CSIRT Greece"); the manual's own redaction of an e-mail
address on the personal-details capture is preserved.

They matter because they are newer than the guidance-page screenshots: the
registration captures no longer show a "Legal name" field.

**Checked against Version 1.1 of October 2026 on 2026-10-13.** ENISA published
the real Version 1.1 on 12 October 2026 with the SRP 1.1 upgrade:
<https://www.enisa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-10/CRA%20SRP%20-%20AR%20User%20Manual%20v1.1.pdf>
(3,289,026 bytes, SHA-256 `2eaea3fc0913a9a9…`, cover "V1.1 / OCTOBER 2026", no
Document History). Each embedded bitmap was compared with the September file's:
nine of the ten are byte-identical in the new PDF, some on a different page
(given below as "v1.1 p. n"). One changed: the manufacturer-details screen,
replaced here by the v1.1 bitmap (PDF page 13, 1402×544). It now labels the
first field "Manufacturer name" and badges both it and Additional Information
"Required", while the manual's text still calls Additional Information optional.
