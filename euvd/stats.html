EUVD statistics

Charts on the EU Vulnerability Database, regenerated every day by the monitor in this repository from the EUVD's own API, the CISA KEV catalogue and Shadowserver honeypot data as served by the EUVD. Data as of 13 Nov 2026. Every panel says what it shows, where the data comes from and what it cannot show. None of it says how a vulnerability reached ENISA: no source records the reporting route. Raw data: stats.json; the exploited entries one by one: index.html; the API: API.md.

This page is pre-rendered: the charts are drawn into the file, so it needs no JavaScript. Hover a bar or dot for its value where your viewer supports it. Each panel can be saved on its own as an image: SVG always, PNG where the browser runs JavaScript.

Time series19. How urgently CISA wants new entries fixed

CISA KEV catalogue (dateAdded, dueDate, forensicTriage), entries added in the last 24 months, by month.

Scroll sideways for the whole chart.

What you seeEach bar splits a month's new entries by the remediation window CISA set for US federal agencies: 3 days, 7, 14, 21. Hollow dots count entries marked for forensic triage: under CISA's directive BOD 26-04 of 10 June 2026 the agency must fix or mitigate the asset within three days and examine it for signs that it has already been broken into. In the last three full months 77% of new entries got three days. Forensic triage first appears in June 2026 and covers 60% of the entries added since. All 103 marked entries carry the 3-day window, which is what the directive ties the mark to. The 21-day window, the old default under BOD 22-01, last appears in March 2026, before the new directive: the move away from it had already begun, so it is not the triage mark that ended it.

Limits: These are deadlines for US federal agencies, not for anyone in the EU; they show how CISA rates urgency, not how dangerous a flaw is in Europe. The triage mark is a duty to look for a compromise, not a finding that one happened, and it says nothing about what anyone in the EU must do. forensicTriage refers to BOD 26-04, which replaced BOD 22-01 on 10 June 2026, and the field is absent for older entries; the windows shown are the ones in the catalogue, which under the directive an agency may have to shorten or lengthen for its own assets.
